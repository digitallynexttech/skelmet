The standard's "second population" prefix is realised here as /account* rather than /portal* — a storefront customer should not be sent to a URL that reads like a vendor portal. proxy.ts fences /account* and /api/account* in both directions, exactly as §6 requires, and session.user.kind still discriminates STAFF from CUSTOMER.
